## Overview

Designed and built a large-scale ERP platform that brings together APIs, warehouse management, EDI integration, automated labeling, and business intelligence. The system handles everything from purchase order receipt to work order management, providing real-time visibility across all manufacturing operations.

## Key Achievements

- Automated workflows reducing manual data entry by 70%
- Integrated barcode scanning system for seamless inventory tracking
- Real-time visibility into production status across multiple facilities
